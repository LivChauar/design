All projects Uber Driver Platform Insurance

Insurance Hub provides Uber Drivers with the information they need in a understandable manner, while also serving as the go-to location for insurance products and related content.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Year
2023–2024
Company
Uber

A redesigned Insurance Hub for Uber Drivers, built around their mental models and real insurance needs — surfacing the right information at the right moment. After years of unchanging design, the Insurance surface had a much needed facelift.

Problem

Albeit fundamental to Uber, the Insurance Hub was over five years old and failed to address both Driver's and Uber's needs

Solution

A new Insurance Hub whose IA and structure are optimized to match the Driver's mental models and provide information while surfacing product offers in the future.

Impact

Currently under development

The problem space

Uber is heavily impacted by Insurance, and insurance cost savings are one of the company's main focus areas. Yet, the Insurance Hub, the main surface for Driver insurance information, was over six years old.

The Insurance team went through a month-long research with Drivers, Uber's internal Insurance team, and our third-party partners, performing shadowing, interviews, co-creation workshops, mapping the end-to-end experience, and identifying areas of improvement.

Users considered Uber's Insurance information too complex to understand and too hard to find.

While we could improve discoverability of insurance information on other surfaces for a holistic approach, giving users a reliable evergreen insurance surface where the information would be simple and easy to use was our next big bet.

Surface goals

Centralize

Centralize entry points for all insurance-related products and information. Users should quickly refer to this page when looking for insurance information.

Emphasize

As insurance information complexity was a major pain point, emphasizing what primary insurances are, and when to use each one of them, was fundamental.

Highlight

With a brand new surface dedicated to insurance-related information, we saw the opportunity to create new points of sale for insurance add-ons and optional insurance products.
