Story

I turn complexity into clarity — and I've been doing it for eight years.

My career has taken me across industries — from redesigning late-payment flows for Telefónica to rethinking the landing process for a São Paulo heliport. What connects every project is the same challenge: taking something genuinely hard to understand and making it feel effortless.

At Uber, I've focused on the driver experience — helping millions of people make sense of their earnings, their safety scores, and their insurance coverage. That means working closely with data scientists, engineers, and researchers to get the details right, not just the surface.

Throughout my career I've led design efforts end-to-end — setting direction, aligning stakeholders, and mentoring junior designers as they find their footing. I believe great design comes from teams that trust each other, and I work hard to build that trust whether I'm leading a project or contributing alongside peers.

I'm drawn to the problems that resist easy answers. The ones that need system thinking, careful research, and a willingness to sit with ambiguity before reaching for solutions. Co-design, service mapping, and high-craft UI are where I feel most at home.
