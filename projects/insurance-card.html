My Role

I was the Lead Product Designer for this project, responsible for leading the vision, managing stakeholders, syncing and aligning across the design org and engineering team.

Led the co-creation workshop with internal and external teams during the discovery phase, and partnered up with the UXR for the interviews, shadowing, roundtables, and user testing.

I also mentored a Design Intern and a Design Apprentice during this project.

Problem space

Uber is heavily impacted by Insurance, and insurance cost savings are one of the company's main focus areas. Yet, the Insurance Hub, the main surface for Driver insurance information, was over six years old.

The Insurance team went through a month-long research with Drivers, Uber's internal Insurance team, and our third-party partners, performing shadowing, interviews, co-creation workshops, mapping the end-to-end experience, and identifying areas of improvement.

For the Insurance Hub, we saw the opportunity to leverage an existing surface and show evergreen insurance information.

Surface goals

  • Centralize entry points for all insurance-related products and information
  • Emphasize what primary insurances are, and when to use each one of them
  • Highlight optional products available for purchase

Exploring the Hub

We explored a variety of hierarchies and visual treatments for the information on Insurance Hub. Keeping with Baseweb's capabilities, we tested the limits of what was feasible on this design system. We also proposed other IAs for the page and aimed at Uber's far future of insurance.

Due to the amount of products available, we decided not to expand the page to include a dedicated, fully-fledged marketplace. Additionally, education was dropped as we couldn't create new learning materials.

The Insurance Card

The insurance card is ingrained into the user's mental model. Frequently cited as being used right off the bat during a crash, we designed a card solution for diverse insurance-related situations.

The final insurance card available on the Insurance Hub emphasizes at which moment of the trip experience insurance was active, the insurance number, and the carrier. Its function is to display information at the hub.

The insurance card would be available at the Insurance Hub by default. However, it was also to be available to drivers at other points of their experience.

The final hub

Finally, we achieved a hierarchy that seemed to implement all the feedback gathered from users during testing sessions.
